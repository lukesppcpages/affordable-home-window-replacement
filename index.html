A few things homeowners ask first

Is Affordable Home the company that installs my windows?+
Affordable Home connects homeowners with a network of licensed, insured, certified local window installers. We are not a contractor ourselves — we handle the matching so you don't have to spend hours researching and vetting companies on your own.
How much will new windows actually cost?+
Cost depends on your home, window count, and style — which is exactly why the in-home estimate is free. You'll get an exact, itemized price before agreeing to anything, plus the current 50% off promotion applied where eligible.
Is the estimate really free, with no obligation?+
Yes. You can get a full estimate, ask every question you have, and decide not to move forward — no cost, no pressure, no catch.
How long does a typical installation take?+
Most standard residential window installations are completed in a single day. Larger projects or specialty styles may take longer — your installer will give you a firm timeline during your estimate.
How does the "no payments for 2 years" offer work?+
Qualified homeowners can finance their project and defer payments for up to 2 years. Your local pro will walk through exact terms and eligibility as part of your free estimate.
